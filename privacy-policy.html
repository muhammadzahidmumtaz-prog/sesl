Privacy Policy — Janu's Used Items Store

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Janu's Used Items Store is a small shop in Lahore that sells second-hand toys and household items through WhatsApp. This page explains what information we collect when you message us, and what we do with it.

What we collect

  • Your WhatsApp phone number and the name shown on your WhatsApp profile.
  • The messages you send us — questions about items, orders, and delivery or pickup choices.
  • Order details: the item you buy, the price, and whether you want delivery or pickup.

How we use it

  • To reply to your questions and confirm your order.
  • To arrange delivery by courier, or pickup.
  • To keep a simple record of orders in a private sheet, so we know what is sold and what is still available.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent, or share your phone number or messages with anyone, except the courier who delivers your order.
  • We do not use your information for advertising.
  • Payment is cash on delivery only. We do not collect card or bank details.

How long we keep it

Order records are kept only as long as needed to complete the sale and handle any question about it. You can ask us at any time to delete your messages and order record, and we will do so.
