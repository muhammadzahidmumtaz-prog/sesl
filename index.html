1. IEE Report Preparation

Initial Environmental Examination reports for projects that need EPA Punjab environmental approval under the IEE category — prepared with consistent area, cost, water and waste figures throughout, the mistakes that most often draw observations.

Price on request · Free initial check

Documents needed — new IEE application on eBiz
  • IEE Report
  • Application form (Schedule IV) with fee receipt / PSID challan
  • Company registration certificate
  • CNIC copy of the applicant
  • Land ownership document, or lease agreement if the land is rented
  • Layout plan
  • KML file of the project site
  • NOC for disposal of wastewater — or a written undertaking if the project produces no wastewater
  • Authority letter for the consultant
  • Estimated project cost

Please note: for a fresh IEE environmental approval, lab reports are NOT needed. Do not spend money on lab testing for a new IEE file unless EPA asks for it later.

2. EIA Report Preparation

Full Environmental Impact Assessment reports for larger projects in the EIA category — including baseline environmental data, impact assessment, mitigation measures and an Environmental Management Plan (EMP).

Price on request · Free initial check

Documents needed — new EIA application on eBiz
  • EIA Report
  • Application form (Schedule IV) with fee receipt / PSID challan
  • Company registration certificate
  • CNIC copy of the applicant
  • Land ownership document, or lease agreement if the land is rented
  • Layout plan
  • KML file of the project site
  • NOC for disposal of wastewater — or a written undertaking if the project produces no wastewater
  • Authority letter for the consultant
  • Estimated project cost
  • Baseline lab reports (air, noise, water and soil, as applicable to the project)

Please note: unlike a fresh IEE, an EIA does need baseline lab reports — they form part of the report's baseline data.

4. Exemption from Environmental Approval

For businesses that were already operating before the promulgation of PEPA, 1997 and are seeking exemption from Environmental Approval on that basis. The proof must be in the company or proponent's own name.

Price on request · Free initial check

Documents needed — exemption application on eBiz
  • Proof that the company was operating before the promulgation of PEPA, 1997 — for example an electricity bill in the company name or the proponent's name from that period
  • Or any other proof from before PEPA, 1997 in the same name — the name on the proof must match the company / proponent
  • Company registration certificate
  • CNIC copy of the applicant
  • Application form with fee receipt / PSID challan

Key point: the pre-1997 proof only works if it is in the company or proponent's name. A bill or record in another person's name will not support the exemption.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an IEE or an EIA for my project?

It depends on your project type and scale. Send us your project details in the free initial check and we will tell you which category your project falls in before you spend anything on a report.

Do I need lab reports for a fresh IEE approval?

No. For a fresh IEE environmental approval, lab reports are not needed. Lab reports are needed for an EIA (as baseline data) and for Confirmation of Compliance cases.

What does the free initial check cover?

We look at your project type, district and the documents you already have, confirm which service and checklist apply to you, and point out anything missing. There is no charge and no obligation to proceed.

How much does a report cost?

Price is on request, because it depends on the project type, size and how complete your documents already are. You receive the price after the free initial check, before any work starts.

My unit was running before 1997. Can I get an exemption?

Possibly — exemption from Environmental Approval requires proof that the company was operating before the promulgation of PEPA, 1997, such as an electricity bill in the company or proponent's name, or another proof in the same name. Send us what you have and we will assess it in the free initial check.

EPA has raised observations on my case. Can you help?

Yes. Send us the observation letter and your submitted report. We prepare a point-wise reply with the revised pages and any additional documents EPA has asked for, ready for resubmission on eBiz.

Who does the SIR / CSR for my project — you or EPA?

EPA does it. While your application for approval is being processed, the SIR / CSR for all projects is done by the field offices of EPA Punjab — it is not a consultant's duty. We cannot prepare, sign or speed up that inspection report. What we do is prepare and file a complete case, track it on eBiz, and help you be ready when the field office visits your site.

Can you guarantee my approval?

No consultancy can. We prepare and file a complete, correct case — the final approval decision is EPA Punjab's. What we can promise is a file that does not fail on avoidable mistakes.
